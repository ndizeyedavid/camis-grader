CAMIS Marks - how to use
========================

This app copies the marks from your Excel file into CAMIS for you.
You need Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on the computer (Edge is on every
Windows 10/11 PC) and an internet connection.


THE 3 STEPS
-----------
1. CHOOSE THE FILE
   Drop your Excel marks file on the app, click to choose it, or click one of
   the recent files the app shows you.

2. OPEN CAMIS
   Click "Open CAMIS". A browser window opens.
   - Log in (only the first time - the app remembers you).
   - Go to Marks recording and open the class and assessment.
   The app notices the student list by itself.

3. FILL MARKS
   The app shows what it will do. If the page has more than one assessment,
   click the right one. Then click "Fill marks". Don't touch the browser while
   it types. It saves when it finishes.

Then look over the marks in CAMIS and submit them when you are happy.
For the next class, click "Fill another file". The browser stays open.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Marks are converted for you. Example: a mark out of 100 in Excel goes into
  a CAMIS column out of 80 as mark x 0.8.
- An empty mark in Excel is entered as 0.
- If CAMIS already has a DIFFERENT mark for a student, the app leaves it alone
  unless you switch on "Replace marks that are different in CAMIS".
- Students the app could not fill are listed at the end with the exact mark to
  type. Do these few by hand.
- You don't have to watch the app. A Windows notification pops up (and the
  app's taskbar button blinks) when the marks page is found, when the marks
  are done, or when something needs you. Turn them off with
  "notifications: on" at the bottom of the app.
- Every run saves a report in Documents\CAMIS Reports
  (click "reports folder" at the bottom of the app).


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "Could not open a browser": install Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
- "Only a few students matched": the browser is showing a different class.
  Open the right class and click "read the page again".
- The browser window was closed by mistake: just click the button again.
- Nothing helps: close the app and open it again. A log for whoever helps you
  is in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CAMIS Marks\app.log
